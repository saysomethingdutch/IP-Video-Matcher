What to label · read before review

PLAYBACK_CONTENT: actual video/programme/content is being presented in a player.

Do not mark search pages, comments, feeds, menus, profile pages, static navigation, or loading/non-playing pages.

Do not split for subtitle changes, programme cuts, brief player controls, very brief buffering, or ordinary seeking while staying in the same player/content context.

Split when leaving the player, entering a clear non-playback section, opening another distinct video, or changing playback context materially.

Seeking inside the same player may remain within one unit. This is capture-side playback presence; do not reconstruct source-video continuity or record source identities.

DOMINANT_VIDEO: programme occupies the dominant region. REACTION_PIP: a person or major overlay shares substantial screen area. OTHER: another playback layout.
